The Secret to Himalayan Apple Galette

Discover the traditional methods of baking with high-altitude fruits, where the air is thinner but the flavors are remarkably dense.

Executive Pastry Chef

Highland Cardamom Tea

Baking at 8,000 feet in the Indian Himalayas presents unique challenges. The lower atmospheric pressure means doughs rise faster, moisture evaporates quicker, and oven temperatures must be adjusted to ensure a golden bake without drying the delicate, paper-thin pastry layers.

Our kitchen uses heirloom Golden Delicious and starking crimson apples harvested straight from the retreat's orchards. We hand-slice them, toss them lightly in brown sugar, freshly ground highland cardamom, and mountain clover honey before arranging them on our signature hand-laminated puff pastry crust.

"The air is thinner up here, but the mountain winds concentrate the sugars in our apples, resulting in a dense flavor profile you cannot find in the valleys below."

We mix locally milled organic grain wheat with cold cultured yak butter. After chilling the dough, we roll it thinly, layering the apples in concentric circles. We bake it at a high heat of 210°C (410°F) to rapidly crisp the pastry, then drop it to 180°C to slowly caramelize the apples to perfection.

Retreat Apple Galette Recipe

Ingredients

  • 2 cups organic mountain grain wheat flour
  • 1 cup cold unsalted yak butter (cubed)
  • 3 large heirloom apples (Golden Delicious)
  • 4 tbsp raw clover honey
  • 1 tsp freshly ground cardamom seeds
  • Pinch of Himalayan pink salt

Prep & Method

  1. Combine flour, salt, and butter; cut until coarse. Add ice water to bind.
  2. Roll out pastry dough to a thin round and chill for 1 hour.
  3. Slice apples thinly and toss with honey and cardamom.
  4. Arrange sliced apples in pastry center, leaving a 2-inch border. Fold edges.
  5. Bake at 210°C for 20 mins, then 180°C for 15 mins until golden brown.
