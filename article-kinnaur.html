The Lost Trails of Kinnaur

Charting the ancient shepherd routes that lead to the most breathtaking vistas of the Inner Himalaya.

Amit Chaudhari

High Altitude Expedition Leader

Published

August 18, 2024

Highest Elevation

11,400 ft

Difficulty Rating

Moderate to Strenuous

Kinnaur, nestled along the ancient Indo-Tibetan Silk Road, is defined by its dramatic rock faces and deep, emerald river gorges. Beyond the motorable highways lie centuries-old shepherd trails, walked only by local semi-nomadic tribes and seasonal herdsmen.

Our travel guide details three of these historic routes: the steep ascent from Karcham to the hanging village of Sapni, the high-altitude meadow trek of alpine pastures beneath the shadow of Kinnaur Kailash, and the riverine paths of Sangla.

"These trails hold the footprints of trade caravans, Buddhist pilgrims, and mountain shepherds whose routes have remained unchanged for millennia."

We advise hikers to travel with local guides, carry high-energy dry apricot rations, and respect the ancient village councils (Devtas) whose cultural laws have protected these peaks since antiquity.
